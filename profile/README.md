# ASCi Consulting

ASCi Consulting is an independent studio for AI-enabled products, internal tools, and research systems.

## What we do

- Product strategy and design engineering
- AI workflow design, automation, and prototypes
- Research systems and data tools

## How we work

We start with the real workflow, make the smallest useful thing, and keep the result understandable and maintainable. Privacy, clear ownership, and evidence-based decisions are built in from the beginning.

## Repositories

This organization is home to selected internal tools, research, and client-facing work. Public repositories are shared deliberately; private repositories remain private by design.
